MY JOURNEY

From offshore operations to advanced marine study.

The experience behind this education goal and the next skills I hope to develop.

My professional background

I am a Nigerian offshore oil and gas professional with more than a decade of experience across rope access, inspection, lifting and rigging, work-at-height supervision and offshore construction support. My work has taken me into demanding industrial environments where safety, teamwork, planning and technical discipline matter every day.

My qualifications and experience include IRATA Level 3 rope access, OPITO rigging, ASNT NDT Level II methods and an HND in Civil Engineering Technology, alongside postgraduate study in project management. These are practical and supervisory credentials; I do not claim professional engineer registration or an existing naval architecture qualification.

Why Naval Architecture, Marine and Ocean Engineering?

Offshore assets operate at the intersection of structures, vessels, machinery, materials, hydrodynamics and complex marine environments. I want to develop a stronger academic foundation in these areas and broaden my ability to contribute to the design, integrity, construction and lifecycle performance of marine and offshore systems.

The MSc in Naval Architecture, Marine and Ocean Engineering at Newcastle University is the programme I am aiming to pursue. Programme availability, entry requirements, tuition fee, scholarship and 2027 admission status must be confirmed directly with the University.

Why I am seeking support

I could not proceed with my intended 2026 study plan because I did not have sufficient funds available for the costs and financial evidence required. This campaign explains the goal, invites sponsorship discussions and will publish supporting information as it is confirmed.

My commitment

If I secure this opportunity, I intend to approach the programme with the discipline and accountability that have shaped my offshore career. I will provide appropriate updates to supporters and keep records of campaign receipts and eligible education-related expenditure.
