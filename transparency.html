ACCOUNTABILITY

Clear information. Responsible use of support.

Supporters deserve to understand what is confirmed, what remains provisional and how contributions would be handled.

What the £50,000 target means

The £50,000 figure is a proposed campaign target only. It is not a confirmed university invoice or verified cost-of-attendance calculation. An itemised budget will be published after official documentation is available.

Intended use of funds

  • Tuition: confirmed tuition balance after any scholarship or fee reduction.
  • Living costs: eligible maintenance costs based on the actual study location and applicable rules.
  • Visa and healthcare: applicable Student visa fee and Immigration Health Surcharge, if required.
  • Travel and arrival: reasonable travel and initial arrival expenses, if included in the final budget.

How funds will be handled

  • Use an approved provider that expressly supports personal education fundraising and Nigerian beneficiary payout.
  • Keep private banking and identity details off this public website and GitHub repository.
  • Maintain records of receipts, transfers and eligible expenditure.
  • Where possible and accepted by the University, explore direct tuition payment arrangements.
  • Provide supporters with reasonable progress and expenditure updates while protecting personal data.

Important UK visa clarification

Crowdfunding proceeds, donor promises and sponsorship letters are not automatically accepted as UK Student visa financial evidence. Check the current UK Visas and Immigration rules, account requirements, holding periods and permitted official sponsorship evidence at the time of application. No contribution guarantees a visa or admission outcome.

Updates

This page should be updated with confirmed offer or application status, current fees, scholarship details, final budget, payment-provider details and periodic reporting as the 2027 study plan develops.
