DUE DILIGENCE

Evidence before assurances.

Prospective sponsors are welcome to request reasonable verification. Documents will be shared through appropriate private channels, with sensitive details redacted where possible.

Documents to confirm and provide

  • Newcastle University application, offer or admission correspondence relevant to the intended 2027 intake.
  • Official programme information and confirmation of availability for the intended intake.
  • Current tuition fee information and confirmed scholarship award or fee reduction.
  • Itemised study budget with sources for each cost.
  • Relevant professional CV and qualification evidence, with certificate numbers and personal identifiers redacted where appropriate.
  • Payment-provider approval and beneficiary verification, once completed.

Privacy and document handling

Identity documents, passport details, bank statements, account numbers, residential address and other sensitive information will not be published in this repository. If due diligence requires a document, request it by email and agree a suitable secure method. Do not rely on screenshots or forwarded messages as a substitute for independent verification with the issuing institution.
